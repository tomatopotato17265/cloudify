About Cloudify

Cloudify is a Minecraft mod that backs up your world saves to your own Google Drive account, so you always have a copy of your world in the cloud without manual exports or third-party file hosts.

Features

  • Log in and out with Google Drive using OAuth
  • Upload singleplayer worlds and instances to a dedicated folder in Google Drive
  • Import worlds and instances from Google Drive, allowing you to seamlessly transfer data across machines or recover any lost data
  • Manage data stored in Google Drive without leaving the game

    • Privacy Policy

    Cloudify collects your email address and manages files in your Drive. Nothing leaves the mod-Drive ecosystem, and everything stays with you. Please read the Privacy Policy for more information.
